# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — first public release (date TBD)

- Live **Memory used** gauge: how full the open chat is, how much is left, and roughly how many steps until Antigravity cleans up by itself.
- **Status strip** in plain words (Plenty of room / Getting full / Almost full) with a one-click **Copy /compact** button.
- **Linked chat banner** that follows whichever chat you have open, with a colour per chat.
- **What's using my memory?** advice box that says, in plain English, whether you need to change anything.
- **Memory breakdown** in five colours: fixed setup, tool results, AI's replies and thinking, your prompts, system notes.
- **Memory over time** chart, with your prompts marked as green dots and the warning levels as dashed lines.
- **Biggest single steps**, each linked to the prompt that caused it. Recognises Maya, Blender, Nuke and Photoshop commands.
- Plain-English hover popups everywhere, with a **Tips: On / Off** switch and a quick guide.
- Follows Antigravity's light and dark theme.
- Strictly read-only. No network access, no dependencies.
