Quick Guide: How the AI's Short-Term Memory Works

Think of each chat as having a desk with limited room. Everything in this chat — your prompts, files the AI opens, tool outputs, and replies — stays on the desk. When the desk fills up, Antigravity summarizes older details to free space.

Prompts vs. AI steps Prompts are messages you type. AI steps are behind-the-scenes actions the agent takes to answer you (reading files, running tools, replying). One prompt often takes 5–15 AI steps!
What 1K means 1K of space is roughly 750 words (about a page and a half). You never need to do math — just follow the green, amber, or red color.
0–59% Plenty of room — Do nothing. You're all good.
60–79% Getting full — Good time to finish your current task.
80%+ Almost full — Type /compact in chat to summarize older steps and keep going here, or start a new chat for a fresh topic.
Short-term memory vs. saved rules (AGENTS.md & Skills)

This meter tracks this chat's temporary desk: everything said or opened in this chat goes onto the desk automatically on every step, and a brand-new chat starts with a clean desk.

Saved across future chats: Antigravity never writes permanent rules or skills on its own — only when you ask it to or run /learn. Put short always-on rules in AGENTS.md (loaded on every chat under Fixed setup), and put multi-step workflows into Skills (only a 1-line title loads until needed, saving desk space).
